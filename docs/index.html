🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 • Developer: Pinto Beans Labs • Application: Kokoro Reader (com.pintobeanslabs.kokororeader)

1. Zero Data Collection & 100% Local Processing

Pinto Beans Labs built Kokoro Reader with an uncompromising privacy-first architecture:

Absolute Data Sovereignty: All text-to-speech inference (via Kokoro-82M and ONNX Runtime Mobile) executes entirely on your device's local CPU/NPU. Your ebooks, documents, PDFs, personal notes, and audio are never transmitted over the internet to any server.
  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your name, email, IP address, device IDs, location, or reading habits.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: Kokoro Reader contains zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising trackers, and zero third-party diagnostic telemetry.
  • No User Accounts: You never have to create an account, log in, or provide credentials to use Kokoro Reader.

2. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

Kokoro Reader requests only the minimal operating system permissions strictly required for offline audio playback and system speech synthesis:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Used exclusively to maintain audiobook playback when your screen is locked, when walking with headphones, or while multitasking in other apps.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents the phone processor from sleeping mid-sentence while reading aloud.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to show playback controls (play, pause, skip sentence) in your lock screen and notification shade.

3. Third-Party App Integration

When you register Kokoro Reader as your system-wide Android TextToSpeechService, other reader apps on your phone (such as Moon+ Reader, ReadEra, or Google Play Books) may pass text to Kokoro Reader for local synthesis. This text is processed immediately in volatile memory and is never recorded, stored, or shared.

4. Children's Privacy

Kokoro Reader does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely offline and safe for users of all ages.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices or Kokoro Reader, please visit our official repository or contact Pinto Beans Labs.
